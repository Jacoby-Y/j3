# Structures and costs

Applied to the game; this file mirrors `src/data/content.j3`. (Ore = raw copper, ingots = smelted.)

## Logistics
- Conveyor: 1 copper ore. 1 cell/s.
- Conveyor Mk2: 1 iron ingot. 3 cells/s.
- Splitter: 2 copper ingots.
- Splitter Mk2: 2 iron ingots.
- Lifter: 2 copper ingots. Rotated it becomes a lowerer.
- Lifter Mk2: 2 iron ingots. Same, lowerer Mk2 when rotated.
- Container: 10 copper ingots. Storage, holds 3 stacks.
- Container Mk2: 10 iron ingots. Storage, holds 6 stacks.

## Production
- Drill: 5 copper ore.
- Furnace: 5 stone. Burns coal.

## Trade
- Market Console: 10 copper ingot.

## Power (CI = copper ingot. II = iron ingot)
- Copper Wire: 1 CI.
- Electric Post: 3 CI + 3 copper wire.
- Coal Generator: 5 II + 5 copper wire. Makes 100 W.
- Electric Drill: 5 II + 5 copper wire. Uses 30 W, twice as fast as the drill.
- Electric Smelter: 5 II + 5 stone. Uses 50 W, no coal, twice as fast as the furnace.

## Sell prices (coins)
- Stone, dirt, grass, raw copper, coal: 1
- Raw iron: 2
- Copper ingot: 3
- Iron ingot: 6
